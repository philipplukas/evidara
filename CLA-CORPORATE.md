## How to sign

Corporate agreements are not signed in a pull-request comment. Send a completed copy of this
document — with the fields below filled in and signed by someone authorized to bind the Corporation
— to the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and say on the pull request that you have done so.

```text
Corporation name:
Corporation address:
Point of contact:
E-mail:
Telephone:

Signed:                                    Date:
Name:
Title:

Schedule A — designated employees authorized to contribute
  (authorization is not tied to particular Contributions)

Schedule B — optional concurrent software grant
  (leave blank or omit if there is no concurrent grant)
```

## What was changed, and why

Against the Apache CCLA (v r190612), this document substitutes the parties ("the Foundation" → "the
Owner"), removes the ASF postal/e-mail submission instructions, replaces the nonprofit undertaking
with section 4 for the same reason given in [CLA.md](CLA.md#what-was-changed-and-why), and reflows
the Schedules as a fill-in block rather than a PDF form. Sections 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 are the
CCLA's sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 with no changes beyond the party substitution.
