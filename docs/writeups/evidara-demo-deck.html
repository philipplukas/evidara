The problem

Trust is the hard part — not search.

Heterogeneous
Every source is different
Federal SPARQL endpoints down to municipal PDFs on ad-hoc sites — one adapter per authority.
Perishable
Freshness is forever
Law changes. A stale legal answer is worse than none — currency must be tracked, not assumed.
Citable
An architecture problem
Being trustworthy enough to put in a brief is about provenance and structure, not ranking.
